# Release Gates

- G0 Requirements: vehicle envelope, battery chemistry, system voltage windows and use cases approved.
- G1 Safety concept: hazards, engine-running interlock, failure states and manual service strategy reviewed.
- G2 Electrical design: power calculations, protection coordination, component derating and thermal model reviewed.
- G3 EVT: engineering samples pass documented functional bench tests.
- G4 DVT: environmental, electrical transient, EMC and abuse tests meet approved limits.
- G5 PVT: production line, calibration, programming, traceability, QC and yield controls demonstrated.
- G6 Release: documentation, regulatory approvals, supplier controls and field-service process approved.

No gate may be marked passed without objective evidence and named reviewer approval.
